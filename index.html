About Us

A studio wired for how technology actually gets built

School Management System

A multi-portal platform for students, teachers, and admins, with secure authentication and role-based access.

View case study

The school had no centralized system for managing student results or applications — everything ran on manual, disconnected processes with no self-service portal for staff, students, or applicants.

FAQ

Common questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Chat with us directly using the button in the corner.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the scope — a landing page costs less than a full business site or a custom web app. Tell us what you need in the "Start a Project" form and we'll give you a clear quote, no guesswork.

How long does a typical project take?

Most business websites take a few weeks from kickoff to launch. Larger builds — like management systems or custom platforms — take longer. We'll give you a realistic timeline once we understand the scope.

Do you offer support after the site is live?

Yes. We stick around for fixes, updates, and the next feature — most of our clients stay with us well past launch.

Can you redesign or upgrade a website I already have?

Yes — we regularly rebuild existing sites on a more scalable, modern foundation rather than starting from scratch unnecessarily.

Do I need coding experience to join the training program?

No. Our web programming training starts from first principles and is built for complete beginners, as well as anyone looking to sharpen existing skills.

Do you work with clients outside Lagos?

Yes — we work with clients remotely across Nigeria and beyond. Most of the process happens over chat, call, and email either way.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you use the "Start a Project" form or the Contact page, we collect the name, email, service selections, and any details you type in. That information is sent to us securely through Web3Forms, a third-party form delivery service, so it reaches our inbox directly. If that delivery ever fails, your email app opens instead with the message pre-filled so you can send it yourself. Either way, we only receive what you choose to submit — nothing is collected as you type.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage for one thing only: remembering whether you prefer light or dark mode. It doesn't track you and isn't shared with anyone. Third-party tools we use (like Web3Forms, Google Analytics, or Calendly, if you use those features) may set their own cookies under their own policies.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they use, so we can improve it. This is aggregate usage data, not individual tracking tied to your identity. You can read Google's own policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Third-party services

Parts of this site rely on third-party services — including Web3Forms for delivering form submissions, Calendly for scheduling calls, and Google Analytics for site usage data. Each operates under its own privacy policy: Web3Forms, Calendly, and Google.

Contact us about privacy

If you have questions about this policy or want any information you've sent us removed, reach out through the Contact page or the phone number in the footer.
