About Us

A studio wired for how technology actually gets built

LxA Nexus is a small, senior team of engineers and designers based in Lagos. We build and modernize websites so they can grow with your business, and we teach the skills behind that work — from web programming to everyday computer literacy — so more people can build for themselves. We embed with your project, ship in weeks not quarters, and stay on to maintain what we build.

Websites, Built to Scale

We build new websites and upgrade existing ones so they hold up as your traffic, content, and features grow — not just at launch.

  • New website builds, from single-page sites to multi-page systems
  • Upgrades to existing sites for speed, structure, and scalability
  • Ongoing maintenance and support after launch

Training & Computer Skills

We teach the skills behind the work — structured web programming packages for people who want to build, and computer appreciation packages for those just getting comfortable with a computer.

  • Web programming packages, from fundamentals to shipping a live project
  • Computer appreciation packages for everyday digital skills
  • One-on-one and small-group formats
